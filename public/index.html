The foundation of wellbeing

Sleep well. Live better.

Sleep is not time lost. It is when the body restores, the mind processes the day and the foundations of tomorrow are built.

A night is a series of cycles

We go through 4 to 6 cycles of about 90 minutes. Each one moves between light, deep and REM sleep. Waking up in deep sleep feels heavy; waking up in light sleep feels natural.

01

What quality sleep gives us

Consistent, restorative sleep supports the systems that keep people engaged, focused and resilient.

  • Better energy, focus and decision-making
  • More stable mood and emotional balance
  • Recovery that helps the body adapt and perform
02

When sleep loses its rhythm

Poor sleep can affect far more than the morning. Repeated disruption makes everyday performance and recovery harder.

  • Morning fatigue and slower mental clarity
  • Lower resilience to stress and daily demands
  • Less consistent energy throughout the day
03

Waking up at the right moment matters

Waking during deep sleep causes sleep inertia: heaviness and disorientation that can last a while. Understanding each person's sleep pattern finds a more natural moment to wake.

How it works

Set it once. It works every night.

  1. 1

    The watch measures sleep

    The user sleeps with their watch on. It records light, deep and REM sleep and saves the night to Health Connect.

  2. 2

    WeSleep learns the pattern

    With the last nights, the algorithm finds when that person's sleep is usually lightest before their alarm.

  3. 3

    It wakes them at the best moment

    Up to 30 minutes before the chosen time. Never later. The app explains why it chose that minute.

Today

The alarm, explained.

A large clock shows when it will ring and the 30-minute window it can use. One switch turns the Smart Alarm on or off, with the days of the week just below.

Your night

Every night, in one look.

A score from 0 to 100, time asleep and the story of the night: each cycle, each stage and the exact moment the alarm rang.

Every morning

How did you wake up?

After the alarm, WeSleep tells the user when and why it woke them, then asks how they feel with a single tap. Then it checks against the watch whether they really were in light sleep.

  • Streaks of mornings woken at the best moment
  • A card to share their morning
  • A daily habit that keeps users coming back
Your week

Trends, not just nights.

A weekly summary with averages, the evolution of hours slept and how regular bedtime is. What really changes how people rest.

  • Average score, efficiency, deep and REM sleep

  • Available in Spanish and Catalan
Reliability

An alarm that always rings.

WeSleep predicts instead of measuring live: the alarm is set before going to sleep, from the learned pattern. It does not depend on the watch sending data during the night.

  • Rings without internetEven if the watch runs out of battery or the server does not respond.
  • Never later than the chosen timeWithout enough data, it rings exactly at the target time.
  • System alarm priorityRescheduled after restarts, time changes and updates.
  • No accounts or passwordsAnonymous sign-up. Users can delete all their data from the app.

A small, stable API

Register a user, upload sleep stages, get the optimal time, insights and morning check-ins. JSON over HTTPS with automatic OpenAPI documentation.

Privacy by design

Anonymous users, a 256-bit key per user stored only as a hash, servers in the EU and a single call to erase everything (GDPR).

Explainable, not a black box

Every prediction comes with its reason. Scores and tips are deterministic and do not issue diagnoses.

  • POST /register
  • POST /sleep/{id}
  • GET /alarm/predict/{id}
  • GET /insights/{id}
  • POST /wake/{id}
  • DELETE /patients/{id}
